## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|------|--------|
| Owner | Everything, including billing, plan changes and managing members |
| Manager | View and edit policies and routes for the instances they can access |

## Seat limits

| Plan | Included members |
|------|------------------|
| Free | 1 |
| Pro | 3 |
| Enterprise | 20 |

Enterprise accounts can add more seats for $1 per user per month. See [Billing & Plans](/docs/dashboard-billing).

If you downgrade, existing members stay on the account, but you can't invite more until you are back under the limit.

## Inviting a member

1. Open **Team** in the dashboard
2. Enter the person's email and choose a role
3. Optionally limit them to specific instances
4. Send the invite. They get an email with a link to accept it

## Limiting access to instances

A member can be scoped to some of your instances. A scoped member:

- can edit the policy of their own instances
- sees the account-level policy as read-only
- can't see the policies of other instances

## Signing in

You can sign in with email and password or with GitHub. If someone signs in with GitHub before accepting an invite, they get a separate account, so have them accept the invite first.

## Custom plan

The Custom plan is designed for large-scale deployments that need more than Enterprise offers. It includes everything in Enterprise, plus unlimited instances, SSO/SAML, audit logs, a dedicated Slack channel, custom SLA, and flexible billing.

To discuss Custom pricing, email [hello@ratelane.dev](mailto:hello@ratelane.dev).

## Managing billing

Billing is managed from **Dashboard → Settings → Plan & Billing**. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Usage limits

If you exceed your plan's instance or saved policy limit, new instances will still connect but policies will not be enforced until you upgrade or reduce usage.
